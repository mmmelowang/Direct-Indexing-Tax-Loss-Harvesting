## How the pieces fit together

1. **Data:** `wrds_loader.py` downloads the data and saves `data/crsp_sp500.pkl`, a `MarketData` object.
2. **Backtests:** `run_experiments.py` loads the data and calls `backtest.py` for each strategy. Every day the engine pays dividends into cash and sells delisted stocks. At each rebalance (at least 31 days apart) it passes the current state (`Context`) to the strategy (`s0.py` or `tam.py`). `tam.py` fits the risk model with `risk.py` and solves for the trades with `optimizer.py`. The engine executes the trades through `lots.py` and pays the tax at each year-end with `tax.py`.
3. **Results:** `report_figures.ipynb` loads `results/`, computes metrics with `metrics.py`, and saves figures and tables to `report/figures/`.

## Strategies and parameters

| Name | Strategy | Risk control | Used in |
|---|---|---|---|
| `S0` | direct index, no harvesting, 10% trim band | – | baseline for tax alpha |
| `S1` | tax-aware optimizer | `gamma_risk = 400` | full period, out-of-sample, windows |
| `S2` | S1 with the deferral-aware tax term | `gamma_risk = 400` | full period, out-of-sample, windows |
| `S1_matched` | S1 at about S2's tracking error | `gamma_risk = 800` | windows |
| frontier S1 / S2 | as above | cap on ex-ante tracking error (0.25%–2%), `gamma_risk = 10` | tracking-error frontier |

Chosen in-sample (2000–2012) by `run_experiments.py select`: k = 15 factors, S0 trim band 10%,
`gamma_risk = 400` (tracking error closest to 0.5%), and `gamma_risk = 800` for S1 matched (tracking error closest to S2's).

## Results files (`code/results/`)

- `select_<run>.pkl`: in-sample (2000–2012) summaries used to choose the parameters above.
- `main_<strategy>.pkl`: full backtest `Result` (daily, rebalance, annual and lot-sale tables), 2000–2025.
- `oos_<strategy>.pkl`: full backtest `Result`, out-of-sample 2013–2025, a new $1M account.
- `window_<year>_<strategy>.pkl`: `metrics.summary` of one 6-year window, January `<year>` to December `<year + 5>`; 21 windows starting 2000–2020.
- `te_full_<strategy>_<cap>.pkl`: `metrics.summary` of the frontier runs, 2000–2025.
